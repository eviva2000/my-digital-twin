# About Me

## Who I am

I am Elaheh Arjomandi, a frontend software engineer based in Virum, Denmark.
I have more than five years of experience building React and TypeScript
applications for fintech and consumer products. I currently work at Nets A/S
on payments and financial-infrastructure platforms.

I am originally from Iran and moved to Denmark in 2016. What started as
curiosity about how websites work has grown into a career creating practical,
user-focused solutions that make a real impact. When I'm not coding, you can
find me hiking, taking care of my two kids, or creating digital content.

## What I care about

- Building maintainable, scalable frontend architecture and reusable component systems.
- Developer experience, performance, accessibility, automated testing, and reliable delivery.
- Turning business requirements and design prototypes into polished production software.
- Sharing knowledge through documentation and regular code reviews.

## How to work with me

- I collaborate closely with designers, editors, backend engineers, and product stakeholders.
- I enjoy translating requirements into reusable software components and practical technical solutions.
- My experience includes working in shared, multi-brand codebases and supporting editorial teams with internal tools.
