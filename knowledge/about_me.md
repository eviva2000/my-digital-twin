# About Me

## Background

I am a frontend software engineer with experience in fintech, financial
infrastructure, and consumer music products. Since June 2022, I have worked at
Nets A/S, where I contribute to a shared, multi-tenant platform built with
Next.js and TypeScript. Before Nets, I worked as a frontend developer at
Moodagent from October 2020 to April 2022.

I hold a BSc in Web Development & Multimedia Design from KEA in Copenhagen
(2017–2021), where I specialized in React, Svelte, and UX/UI design principles.
I also hold a BSc in Computer Science from Azad University in Tehran
(2010–2014), covering Java programming, computer architecture, and core
computer-science concepts.

## Skills

- Frontend: TypeScript, JavaScript, React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, TanStack Query, Redux, GraphQL, and REST.
- Backend and data: Node.js with Express, Prisma, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Supabase, and Neon PostgreSQL.
- Quality and delivery: Playwright, Vitest, Storybook, axe accessibility testing, GitHub Actions, CI/CD, Vercel, and Git.
- AI: OpenAI API and AI-assisted application development.

## Projects

- DevStash: a full-stack AI developer knowledge hub built with Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Auth.js, Vitest, the OpenAI API, and Vercel.
- Wild Oasis customer website: a booking flow with authentication built with Next.js, TanStack Query, Supabase, NextAuth.js, and Tailwind CSS.
- Wild Oasis management website: a full-stack live management dashboard with filtering, mutations, and server-state handling using React, TanStack Query, Supabase, NextAuth.js, and Tailwind CSS.

## Preferences and goals

I focus on frontend work where reusable architecture, strong developer
experience, performance, accessibility, and product quality matter. I enjoy
working across design, content, engineering, and product to deliver reliable
customer-facing applications.
